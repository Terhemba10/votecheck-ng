# Demo video script (about 3 minutes)

Record the live site: **https://votecheck-ng.onrender.com**. Open it once a minute before you record, so the free host is awake. Speak slowly and let each screen settle before you talk about it. Times are a guide.

**Before you start:**
- Use a fresh browser window (a private/incognito window works), so the language chooser appears.
- Zoom the browser to 110–125% so text is readable on video.
- On Windows, **Win + Alt + R** starts and stops recording (Xbox Game Bar). Trim the video in **Clipchamp**.

---

## 1. Opening and language (0:00–0:25)
**Show:** the language chooser on first load.
**Say:** "This is VoteCheck NG, an independent, nonpartisan tool for Nigerian elections. The first thing a voter sees is a choice of language: English, Pidgin, Hausa, Yorùbá or Igbo."
**Do:** click **Hausa**. Pause a second on the home page.
**Say:** "Choose Hausa, and the whole site switches: menus, pages, forms, results and the chatbot."
**Do:** use the picker at the top to switch back to **English**.

## 2. Voter education (0:25–0:45)
**Show:** the animated scenes. Let them turn once, or click the small bars.
**Say:** "Three messages for voters: your vote will count, vote your choice, and don't sell your vote. The characters wear Hausa, Igbo and Yorùbá dress, and the ballot uses placeholder parties, so nothing here favours anyone."

## 3. Find my polling unit (0:45–1:10)
**Do:** click **Find my polling unit**. Click the test VIN `00TEST0000000000002`.
**Say:** "Voters can look up where they vote. This is a demo on fictional test voters, clearly labelled, because real VIN lookups need INEC's written authorisation. Once INEC approves, this same box connects to INEC's register. We never store anyone's VIN or NIN."

## 4. Ask VoteCheck (1:10–1:35)
**Do:** open **Ask VoteCheck**, switch the language to **Yorùbá**, and click a suggested question.
**Say:** "The assistant answers questions about registration, PVCs, voting day and results, in the voter's own language."
**Do:** switch back to **English** and type "Who should I vote for?"
**Say:** "It never tells anyone who to vote for. It explains how to compare candidates fairly."

## 5. Check results (1:35–2:20)
**Do:** open **Check results**. Point to the tiles and the first flagged rows.
**Say:** "Observers upload polling-unit results. Fixed rules compare each paper result sheet with its IReV upload. Here, unit 01-003's sheet shows 149 votes for Party B but IReV shows 194, so it's flagged. A flag means 'verify this', never 'fraud'."
**Do:** press **Write report** and show the first lines.
**Say:** "It writes an observer report with verification steps, in any of the five languages."
**Do:** scroll down to the **2023 presidential election** section.
**Say:** "We also tested the rules on real public data. Here are INEC's declared totals for the 2023 presidential election, and a published state-by-state copy of the results. Our checks found that this copy doesn't add up: the states sum to over ten thousand more votes for one candidate than declared, and two state rows don't add up internally. That's exactly the kind of gap observers need to verify against INEC's official collation sheets."

## 6. Check a result sheet (2:20–2:45)
**Do:** open **Check a result sheet** and click **Fill in a fictional example**.
**Say:** "An observer can go through one Form EC8A: the figures, the presiding officer's signature and stamp, and the times. Here, the stamp is missing, and the IReV upload time is earlier than the time the sheet was completed."
**Do:** tick **The sheet has the official stamp**, and show the list getting shorter.
**Say:** "It's a checklist for people. It never decides whether a sheet is valid; only INEC and the courts do that."

## 7. Responsible AI and close (2:45–3:05)
**Show:** the home page.
**Say:** "Our principle: rules decide the flags, people decide the outcome, and AI only explains. Every rule is covered by automated tests, the site stores no personal data, and it keeps working even if the AI service is down. VoteCheck NG: know how to vote, and check that results add up. Thank you."

---

### If something goes wrong while recording
- **The site is slow on the first click:** the free host was asleep. Wait, then record again.
- **The language chooser doesn't appear:** you've visited before in that browser. Use a private window.
- **A page shows a different language from what you expect:** use the picker at the top right.
